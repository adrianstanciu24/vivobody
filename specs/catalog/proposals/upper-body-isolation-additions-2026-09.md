# Upper-body isolation additions

Status: Active catalog additions. Reviewed September 18, 2026.

## Outcome and scope

Added six distinct fixtures to the existing elbow-extension, elbow-flexion and
reverse-fly families. Duplicate search found no matching authored setup. The
owner requested these exact additions, authorizing their narrow local contract
expansions. No shared schema, taxonomy, anatomical capability or persistence
change was needed. Existing exercise identities and saved snapshots remain intact.

| Exercise | Exact fixture | Load entry |
|---|---|---|
| Overhead Rope Cable Triceps Extension | Standing, high pulley behind athlete, split stance, slight forward lean, both rope ends, upper arms beside head | One stack setting, same station and attachment |
| Two-Handed Dumbbell Overhead Triceps Extension | Seated without back support, both hands under one dumbbell head, upper arms overhead | Full marked weight of that single dumbbell once |
| Cable Rear-Delt Fly | Standing, opposite handles crossed, overhand grip, shoulder-height arc, fixed slight elbow bend | One of two equal stack settings, same fixture |
| Bent-Over Dumbbell Reverse Fly | Unsupported hip hinge, near-horizontal torso, neutral grip, held elbow angle | One dumbbell from the matching pair |
| Dumbbell Concentration Curl | Seated, working upper arm braced against inner thigh, held supinated grip | One dumbbell; repeat the set on the other side |
| Single-Arm Dumbbell Preacher Curl | Seated, upper arm on inclined preacher pad, held supinated grip; pad angle unreported | One dumbbell; repeat the set on the other side |

## Contract and independent review

Three read-only discovery lanes reviewed exercise pairs and cross-family
boundaries. Reviewers then exchanged drafts so they did not approve their own
proposals. Evidence and contract reviews passed after correcting source-author
metadata and rejecting cable-only fields on non-cable flyes and unrelated
new torso-angle data on existing rope pushdowns.

Exact fixture rules reject unsupported support/grip/laterality combinations.
Reverse flyes retain held elbows and no deliberate scapular-retraction or
external-rotation prime action. Triceps extensions retain elbow extension alone;
curls retain elbow flexion alone. The original Maeo cable attachment remains
unreported. No source-defined unknown was silently assigned a precise angle.
