## Shared admission decision

The first six movements are mobility-intended in this request, including partner-assisted and prop-supported range-of-motion work. The source also names shoulder stability and core strength among Downward Dog with Toe Touch's aims. Vivobody's active modalities are `dynamicStrength`, `isometricStrength`, and `power`. The two strength modalities contribute to strength records and hard-set muscle analytics. Encoding these drills as strength merely because they have repetitions or a hold would misstate their training effect. In particular, Downward Dog with Toe Touch uses a high-plank support position, but the source defines the hip pike, straight-leg position, and opposite-toe reach as the movement; shoulder and core bracing are support demands alongside the mobility task. This differs from the adjacent Plank Row w/ Knee Touch, whose source explicitly makes anti-extension, anti-rotation, and shoulder stability the training task. A `mobility` modality needs an owner-approved shared product, schema, and runtime contract: display/search classification, repetition or duration logging, history, records, comparison, substitution, routine selection, and exclusion from strength volume and load analytics. Do not infer achieved range-of-motion improvement or pain/injury prevention from the source's coaching prose. The existing `lumbarExtensors` region cannot serve as a named thoracic extensor region.

Each mobility movement would need its own narrow family if a future product decision admits it. Machine torso rotation, MedX lumbar extension, high-plank rotation, and high-plank knee touch do not own these fixtures. The six mobility names have no active catalog rows under the current strength-only decision.

## Punch ISO Holds: straight variant active, hook and uppercut pending

The PDF describes partner-resisted straight, hook, and uppercut punch-position holds, 5–10 seconds per variation. A jab is held near full reach and a hook at mid range. A separate [publisher protocol](https://boxingscience.co.uk/10-exercises-to-improve-punch-snap-for-boxing/) uses a 20–30-second sequence, changing punch angle every 3–5 seconds; it is not the same logging unit. These are genuine `isometricStrength` tasks with duration and non-comparable manual resistance, but the plural name does not define one interchangeable shoulder/elbow/torso position. [Measured jab and hook kinematics](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3377096/) support keeping punch topologies separate; that moving-punch study does not measure these static holds or their muscle contributions. The existing bilateral wall press explicitly excludes single-arm punch holds, and landmine punch exercises have a different implement path.

The owner selected the straight hold first. `partner-resisted-straight-punch-hold.json` authors one near-full-reach straight position, either arm in a separate timed set, a planted boxing stance, and partner palm-to-fist pressure back along the punch line. Stance, fist height, contact, and pressure direction are bounded catalog choices because page 16 states the near-full jab range but depicts a bent-arm hold. They are not source-measured joint angles or forces. The generic `Punch ISO Holds` name and `Jab ISO Hold` remain unclaimed aliases: the former includes hooks and uppercuts, while jab specifically implies the lead arm.

**Unlock for hook and uppercut:** define each hold's stance, working side, fist/partner contact, joint angles or reproducible end position, resistance direction, and whether one timed set represents one punch on one side. Then author separate exact fixtures or narrowly ruled families. The active straight family follows the `multidirectional-manual-core-hold` precedent: `bodyweight` plus `nonComparable` hides false numeric resistance while the name and instructions state that a partner is required. Do not assign a fixed external weight or claim a measured force from partner effort. Workout history does not persist which arm completed each timed set, so the side instruction cannot prove balanced sides from records.
