The named source section is *Mobility*, but Vivobody's active catalog tracks rep-counted strength work rather than mobility outcomes. A source label alone does not authorize a new modality or a claim that a drill improves range of motion.

## Lunge Matrix

Page 32 names forward, lateral, and reverse-diagonal lunges and an eye-and-hand reach. The publisher's demonstration shows discrete steps forward, sideways, and rearward on a diagonal with a hand reach toward the step, returning to centered standing between directions. The rearward crossing angle, side order, and one-repetition unit remain unreported. The complete sequence crosses the active sagittal dynamic-lunge and frontal lateral-lunge boundaries.

**Unlock:** establish an explicit side prescription and logged repetition unit, then decide whether the demonstrated warmup drill has an honest rep-counted strength interpretation under the current product contract. A separate multi-plane complex family would be required; a simple lunge alias would erase the defining directions and reach.
