# Bodyweight plus added-load logging

- Status: Active owner-approved catalog setting
- Scope: The 29 exercises below; other records retain their current settings

Use the existing Pull-Up load control. Zero added weight displays BW. Positive
added weight displays BW plus the entered weight. Keep each exercise's reps or
time setting. Do not require a load-implement selection.

Each record uses `bodyweightAdded`, coefficient `1`, and default added weight
`0`. This is the owner's body-mass logging convention. It is not a measured
lifted-body fraction or force model. Original geometry evidence supports the
movement instructions and categorical anatomy. It does not establish universal
resistance fractions for supported or limb movements.

The compiler and runtime allow only the named ab-wheel and six TRX exceptions.
Bands, stability balls, and the other TRX fixtures retain their restrictions.
Custom copies of approved records retain the source load setting.

The existing load mode supplies strength records, tonnage, and estimated 1RM
for dynamic-strength repetitions with known measured body weight. Timed strength
ranks load first, then duration. Power remains outside strength records and
estimated 1RM. Saved workouts and templates retain their original load settings.
Current Detail records do not reinterpret old snapshots. Time-only and loaded
hold records have separate standing records.
