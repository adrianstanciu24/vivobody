# Adductor coverage

Status: active catalog amendment, 2026-09-08. Independent evidence, boundary,
and product reviews completed; runtime evidence is recorded in the task handoff.

## Active decisions

| Fixture | Authored roles and boundary |
|---|---|
| Supported Standing Band Hip Adduction | Longus/brevis primary; gracilis, magnus and pectineus secondary. Existing geometry retained. |
| Technogym Seated Hip Adduction Machine | Same roles, retaining the exact supported 80-degree hip-flexion machine fixture. |
| Supported Standing Cable Hip Adduction | New open-chain branch: low pulley, nearer ankle cuff, nearer hand on the upright, straight working knee, held slight hip flexion for foot clearance. External stack load, unilateral repetitions. |
| 60%-Height Bodyweight Lateral Lunge | Add magnus, gracilis and pectineus secondaries for the hip-adduction return. This role is supported by the adduction return. |
| Partner-Supported Copenhagen Adduction | New dynamic family: partner supports upper straight leg under both ankle and knee, one forearm grounded, lower leg meets upper leg at the top. Longus/brevis primary; gracilis, magnus and pectineus secondary. |

All five requested surfaces already exist. Longus and brevis remain a single
role region covering two meshes. Primary, secondary and stabilizer assignments
affect analytics as well as coloring; no display-only roles are introduced.
Existing workout and template snapshots are not retroactively rewritten.

| [Harøy 2017](https://doi.org/10.1177/0363546517720194) | Video supplement specifies partner support at both ankle and knee, body raise with lower-leg adduction, and controlled halfway descent without lower-foot support. | Does not establish an ankle-only bench version, individual muscle forces or a bodyweight fraction. |

Riemann's existing lateral-lunge source specifies lateral displacement and
measures sagittal mechanics. The frontal return and added adductor roles remain
explicit anatomy-and-mechanics inferences, not newly measured frontal moments.
Copenhagen shoulder/scapular/elbow stabilizers are support-chain inferences;
the lower foot is not a weight-bearing contact, so side-plank calf roles are
not copied into this family. Its noncomparable bodyweight load remains zero;
unilateral identifies one support-side configuration per set, not exclusive
activation of one leg. Default loads and repetitions are initialization choices.

## Gates and retained limits

The existing band and machine records are retained without duplication. Cable
expands the local hip-adduction contract; each equipment branch pins its own
posture and support so standing-machine hybrids cannot bypass the rules.
Copenhagen has a distinct family; ordinary hip adduction and side plank retain
their neighboring exclusions. No taxonomy or schema changes. The shared magnus profile remains adduction-only.

Magnus now receives secondary credit on Barbell Back Squat, Conventional
Barbell Deadlift, and Barefoot Dead-Stop Sumo Barbell Deadlift. The latter
changes from stabilizer to secondary; bodyweight bridge remains stabilizer.
The compiler grants portion-qualified extension participation only to these
three fixture IDs, in their reviewed families, with secondary roles and both
portion-evidence references. No posture threshold or whole-muscle extension
capability is invented. Exercise Detail has a collapsed explanation of the
back-portion contribution and the nonuniform meaning of its highlight.

[Benn 2018](https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.23068) directly sampled two magnus
regions during resisted extension; its 0/45/90-degree positions concern
adduction tests, not an extension-angle threshold.
[Collings 2026](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000004002) estimates
ischiocondylar-magnus force during squat, conventional and sumo deadlift with
a neuromusculoskeletal model. It does not measure muscle force directly or
establish primary rankings. These provide categorical mechanics transfers to
the unchanged exact fixtures. Hanen's existing source adds direct conventional
and sumo magnus EMG but does not alone establish a prime-force role.
Other squat and deadlift variants require separate review.

Bench-only Copenhagen, short-lever, timed-hold, eccentric-only and externally
loaded versions require separate fixture review. Existing adductor roles are
not promoted to primary, and no generic stabilizer expansion is claimed.
