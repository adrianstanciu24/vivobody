Frequently asked

How do I restore my Vivobody Pro purchase?

Open Settings in the app, tap Unlock Vivobody Pro, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

How do I save workouts to Apple Health?

Apple Health sync is part of Vivobody Pro. Turn it on in Settings > Apple Health, then grant write permission when iOS asks. Finished workouts appear in the Health app automatically. Vivobody never reads your Health data.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts and no servers. See the privacy policy for details.

I deleted the app. Can I get my workouts back?

If you restore your iPhone from a backup made while the app was installed, your data comes back with it. Otherwise deleted data cannot be recovered, since it only ever existed on your device.
