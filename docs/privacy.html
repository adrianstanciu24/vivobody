Privacy Policy

Vivobody · Effective July 27, 2026

The short version: Vivobody stores everything on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Your data never leaves your iPhone unless you explicitly save workouts to Apple Health.

Data we collect

None. Vivobody has no backend and collects no data. We cannot see your workouts, your body weight, or anything else you enter in the app.

Where your data lives

Workouts, templates, exercise catalog edits, body weight entries, and settings are stored locally on your device in the app's private database. Widget data is a small local snapshot shared between the app and its widgets through Apple's App Group container, also entirely on your device. If you use iCloud device backups, your Vivobody data is included in your own encrypted backup, which we have no access to.

Apple Health

Saving workouts to Apple Health is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, Vivobody asks for write-only permission and saves each finished workout to the Health app. Vivobody never reads any data from Apple Health, and your health data is never transmitted anywhere by Vivobody. You can revoke access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health, and manage or delete saved workouts in the Health app.

Purchases

Vivobody Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchase verification happens on your device using Apple's StoreKit; no purchase information is sent to us.

Notifications

Rest timer alerts use local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification service is used.

Siri, Spotlight, and widgets

Shortcuts, Spotlight search entries, and widget content are generated and stored on your device by Apple's system frameworks. They are not shared with us or any third party.

Third parties

Vivobody contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, and no advertising. The only parties involved are you and Apple's operating system.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all Vivobody data from your device. Workouts previously saved to Apple Health remain in the Health app until you remove them there.

Children

Vivobody is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Since the app collects no data, changes are expected to be rare.

Questions about privacy? Email vivobodyapp@gmail.com.
